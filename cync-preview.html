Terms of Use

Last updated November 8, 2026

Cync on Sonic

Cync is a service run by Avenor on Sonic mainnet. Payments use real assets and cannot be reversed. Cync's contracts are public and ready for an external audit. Like all smart contracts, they may contain bugs. The service can change, break, or stop at any time.

Your wallet and your transactions

Cync never holds your private keys and cannot sign for you. Every transaction is created for your review and signed in your own wallet. You are responsible for checking the recipient, token, and amount before you sign. Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed.

Links made by other people

Anyone can create a Cync link. Titles, descriptions, and images come from the link creator and are not reviewed by Avenor. A link marked Unverified has not been checked, and a creator signature only shows which wallet signed the link, not that its owner can be trusted. Only pay links from sources you trust.

Fees

Payments through the Cync router may include a platform fee. The fee is shown on the action card before you sign.

Acceptable use

Do not use Cync for scams, phishing, impersonation, or anything unlawful, and do not try to disrupt the service. You are responsible for following the laws that apply to you.

No warranty

Cync is provided as is, without warranties of any kind. To the extent the law allows, Avenor is not liable for losses that result from using the service, including mistaken transactions, smart contract faults, and network or wallet failures.

Changes and contact

These terms may change, and the date above shows the latest version. Questions: avenor.protocol@gmail.com

Privacy

Last updated November 8, 2026

What Cync does not collect

Cync has no accounts and asks for no name or email. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics of its own.

Requests to our server

To build a link preview or a transaction, the server receives the details in the link and, when you pay, your wallet address. Your IP address is kept in memory for rate limiting only and is not written to request logs.

Your browser

Your browser keeps your wallet connection in local storage so you stay connected. You can clear it at any time.

Public blockchain

Transactions are public and permanent on the blockchain, including wallet addresses and amounts.

Other services

These services receive your IP address and the data needed to do their job, under their own privacy policies:

  • WalletConnect (Reown), when you connect a wallet through it. It also collects usage statistics.
  • The Sonic network RPC, which your browser contacts to read balances and fees.
  • The image host a link creator chose, when a link uses a custom image.
  • The block explorer, if you open a transaction there.
